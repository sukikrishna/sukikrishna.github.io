Beyond Research

Outside of school and tech, I love trying new things and saying yes to a little adventure. In undergrad that meant starting to learn Korean; since moving to Boston, it's been figure skating (badly) and climbing (even more badly). Most of what's below I do casually, for the fun of learning something new or spending time with friends.
